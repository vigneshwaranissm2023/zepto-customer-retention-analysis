# 📊 Evaluating the Effectiveness of Free Cash Offers on Customer Retention in Zepto's Loyalty Program in Chennai

## 📌 Project Overview

This MBA research project analyzes the impact of free cash offers, loyalty benefits, and promotional rewards on customer retention in Zepto's loyalty program. The study focuses on understanding how different loyalty program components influence customer engagement, repeat purchases, and long-term retention among users in Chennai.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Evaluate the effectiveness of free cash offers on customer retention.
* Analyze the impact of discounts and promotional offers.
* Study the influence of referral bonuses on customer engagement.
* Examine how personalized rewards affect customer loyalty.
* Assess the contribution of subscription benefits to repeat purchases.

## 📈 Key Findings

* Free cash offers positively influence customer retention and repeat purchases.
* Discounts and promotions increase customer engagement.
* Personalized rewards improve customer satisfaction and loyalty.
* Subscription benefits contribute to long-term retention.
* Loyalty program participation reduces customer switching behavior.
* User experience and reward attractiveness significantly impact retention.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

* Introduce more personalized rewards.
* Enhance loyalty program awareness among users.
* Implement tier-based reward systems.
* Improve communication regarding loyalty benefits.
* Offer exclusive benefits for frequent customers.

---

## ✅ Conclusion

The study concludes that free cash offers, promotional rewards, personalized incentives, and subscription benefits have a significant positive impact on customer retention. Businesses can improve customer loyalty and repeat purchase behavior by implementing targeted reward strategies and enhancing the overall customer experience.
